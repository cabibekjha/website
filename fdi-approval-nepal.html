FDI approval and foreign investment in Nepal

विदेशी लगानी (FDI) स्वीकृति

Bring foreign investment into Nepal the right way, from approval to repatriating your returns.

Book a consultation WhatsApp me

The FDI process, step by step

  1. Check the sector and structure. Confirm your sector is open to foreign investment and plan the shareholding and funding structure.
  2. Foreign investment approval. Apply to the Department of Industry, which approves all foreign investment.
  3. Register the company at the Office of the Company Registrar.
  4. Bring in the investment through banking channels and record it with Nepal Rastra Bank.
  5. Post-registration. PAN and VAT registration, industry registration where needed, and ongoing compliance.
  6. Repatriation. Dividends and capital can be repatriated after tax, with Nepal Rastra Bank approval.

Frequently asked questions

What is the minimum foreign investment in Nepal?

Under current government policy, NPR 2 crore (20 million) per investor, with no minimum for IT companies. The figure is set by government decision and can change, so it should be confirmed before you apply.

Are any sectors closed to foreign investment?

Yes. FITTA includes a list of sectors where foreign investment isn't allowed. Your sector is checked against it before anything else.

Can I take profits out of Nepal?

Yes. After paying applicable taxes, dividends and capital can be repatriated with approval from Nepal Rastra Bank, provided the investment was properly recorded.
