Frequently asked questions

Do I need to register my employees with SSF?

Employers covered by the Social Security Act must register with the Social Security Fund and contribute monthly: 11% of basic salary from the employee and 20% from the employer.

How is tax on salary calculated in Nepal?

Salary tax uses progressive slabs, with separate slabs for individuals and couples. The employer deducts it monthly and deposits it within 25 days after the month ends.

Can you handle payroll for foreign employees?

Yes, including tax residency, foreign allowances and payments across countries.
